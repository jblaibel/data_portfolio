### Business Question:

How has sales performance changed over time, and which product categories and customer regions generate the most revenue?

### Metrics we care about:
- Revenue
- Order volume
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Growth over time

### Data Model Overview:

The dataset consists of customer orders, where each order may contain multiple items. Orders are linked to customers and products through unique identifiers. Revenue is calculated at the order item level and aggregated across time, product categories, and customer regions for analysis.

One order could have many order items.
each item has a price
Products map to categories
Customers map to locations.

### Key Insights
- Revenue grew consistently over time with seasonal effects.
- A small set of product categories generated most revenue.
- Sales were concentrated in a few geographic regions.

### Recommendations
- Focus marketing and inventory on high-performing categories.
- Tailor regional strategies for underperforming areas.
